## The problem I ran into & how I solved it
* **What I was trying to do:** Getting the 3D Floating HUD window to recognize speech hands-free like the web dashboard.
* **The error:** Speech was not recognized in the floating HUD window due to noise floor miscalibration and missing secure context.
* **What actually caused it:** Ambient noise calibration clamped energy threshold to 400, whereas physical mic noise floor is ~930–1200 RMS, causing speech to time out continuously. Additionally, ile:/// loading in WebView2 lacked a secure context for Web Audio.
* **How I fixed it:** Served the HUD via an embedded HTTP server on port 8088 (http://127.0.0.1), upgraded device selection to prioritize physical Intel Smart Sound arrays (Device 2), enabled dynamic energy threshold adaptation, and added synchronous Groq Whisper STT with hallucination filtering.
* **Takeaway / lesson learned:** Dynamic acoustic environments require adaptive thresholding above the natural room noise floor rather than arbitrary hardcoded limits.
