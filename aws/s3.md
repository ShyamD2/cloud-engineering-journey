# Amazon S3 Notes & Best Practices

S3 is simple to get started with, but there are a few security and cost traps worth keeping in mind.

---

## Golden Rules for Every New Bucket
1. **Block Public Access (BPA):** Always turn on all 4 checkboxes unless you are intentionally hosting a public website.
2. **Enforce HTTPS:** Add a bucket policy condition requiring `aws:SecureTransport: true`.
3. **Turn on Versioning:** Saves you from accidental `rm -rf` or code bugs overwriting important data.
4. **Default Encryption:** Use SSE-S3 or SSE-KMS so unencrypted uploads are impossible.

---

## Cost Optimization with Lifecycle Rules
* **Application logs:** Move to `Standard-IA` after 30 days, transition to `Glacier Flexible Archive` after 90 days, and expire after 365 days.
* **Small files trap:** Standard-IA has a minimum billable object size of 128KB. Moving thousands of tiny 2KB files to IA will actually increase your bill because you get billed for 128KB anyway!
